Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 6, 2026

BackTracker - Recovery Log ("the App") is developed by Rodney Hughey ("we", "our", "us"). Your privacy is critically important to us — especially when it comes to your health data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. All data you enter into the App — including pain entries, exercise logs, daily check-ins, recovery milestones, and progress photos — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We have no servers and no way to access your data.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App may read and write the following data types to and from Apple Health (HealthKit):

  • Steps — to track your daily activity as part of your recovery
  • Active Energy — to monitor energy expenditure during recovery

This data is exchanged directly between the App and Apple Health on your device. We never have access to your HealthKit data. The App does not store HealthKit data separately — it reads from and writes to Apple Health in real time. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BackTracker.

Camera and Progress Photos

With your explicit permission, the App may access your device camera to capture progress photos documenting your recovery. These photos are stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or have any access to your photos. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a progress photo — never on app launch or in the background.

Health and Medical Data

The App allows you to log sensitive health-related information including pain levels, body locations, mobility assessments, mood, and physical therapy exercises. All of this data is stored exclusively on your device. We have no ability to view, access, or retrieve your health entries. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Subscriptions

The App offers optional subscriptions managed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or process any payment information. All subscription transactions are handled by Apple. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple handles your purchase data.

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or device information. There are no cookies, no fingerprinting, and no behavioral tracking of any kind.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third parties. Period.

Data Export

The App provides features to export your recovery data. These exports are generated locally on your device and shared using the standard iOS share sheet. We do not receive copies of your exported data. You may choose to share exports with your doctor or physical therapist at your discretion.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. Deleting the App will remove all stored data. There is nothing on our end to delete because we never had your data to begin with.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
