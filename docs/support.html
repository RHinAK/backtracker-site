Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log my daily check-in?

Open the app and you'll see the Dashboard. If you haven't checked in today, a check-in prompt will appear. Rate your current mobility, pain level, and mood, then tap Save. It takes about 30 seconds and gives you a daily snapshot of your recovery.

How does pain tracking work?

In the Pain Journal, tap the + button to add a new entry. Rate your pain from 1-10, select the body location where you're feeling pain, and add optional notes about what you were doing or what might have triggered it. Over time, your pain charts will show trends and patterns.

What's in the PT exercise library?

BackTracker includes a curated library of physical therapy exercises commonly prescribed for back surgery recovery. Browse by category — stretching, strengthening, and mobility. Each exercise includes instructions. Log your completed exercises to track consistency. Always follow your physical therapist's specific recommendations.

What is the Recovery Timeline?

The Recovery Timeline is a visual history of your entire recovery journey. It shows your pain trends, exercise activity, daily check-ins, and milestones plotted over time. It's a powerful way to see progress that might not be obvious day-to-day — especially useful for sharing with your care team.

Is my data private?

Yes — completely. All your data is stored locally on your device. We don't have servers, we don't collect your data, and we never sell anything to anyone. Your pain logs, check-ins, photos, and exercises stay on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How does Apple Health integration work?

When you grant permission, BackTracker can read your daily steps and active energy from Apple Health, and write data back. This helps you see how your activity levels relate to your recovery progress. The data exchange happens directly on your device — nothing is sent to us.

How do progress photos work?

Go to the progress photos section and use your camera to take a photo. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. Use them to visually track your posture, mobility, and physical changes over your recovery.

Can I share my data with my doctor or PT?

Yes! Use the data export feature to generate a report of your recovery data. You can share it via email, AirDrop, or any other method using the standard iOS share sheet. This is a great way to give your care team insight into your day-to-day recovery between appointments.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find BackTracker, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

I'm not seeing my subscription / features are locked

Try these steps: (1) Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. (2) Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases." (3) Restart the app.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your data is stored locally, so deleting the app will remove your data. We strongly recommend exporting your data before uninstalling. If you've synced with Apple Health, your steps and activity data will still be available there.
